The operating system Cowork should become

Cowork acts as the Chief of Staff when it can understand context, watch for commitments and risk, then act by preparing briefs, drafts, and recommendations. The Executive stays in control of judgment calls and external commitments.

Step 1

Writing the Chief of Staff Constitution

The constitution is the system prompt for the Executive's operating system. It tells Cowork what to optimize for, how to make tradeoffs, when to be candid, and where it must stop and ask for approval. Without this, Cowork will summarize activity. With it, Cowork can recommend action.

Where to go in Cowork: open Cowork, create a new chat, name it Chief of Staff OS, paste the prompt below, and ask Cowork to confirm how it will operate.

Why it matters: this establishes Cowork as an executive operating partner, not a passive summarizer.

            Step 2
            
Pick the first jobs Cowork should own

            
Do not ask Cowork to run the whole office on day one. Choose a few recurring, high-leverage jobs that create trust quickly: controlling the day, preparing for important meetings, and making sure follow-through happens.

            
Where to go in Cowork: stay in the Chief of Staff OS thread. Paste each job prompt one at a time. Ask Cowork what information or permissions it needs before making the job recurring.

            Step 3
            
Install the first operating rhythm

            
The first meeting should end with a repeatable loop, not just good prompts. Convert the jobs into a daily and meeting-based rhythm so Cowork watches for the right things at the right time.

            
Where to go in Cowork: use Cowork's automation or recurring workflow area for scheduled watch loops. Use the Chief of Staff OS thread as the home base and save the best prompts as reusable skills or templates when available.

            Improve the OS
            
Hill climb from useful to indispensable

            
The goal is not to design the perfect Chief of Staff up front. The goal is to run one loop, learn what was wrong, tighten the constitution, and add the next job only when the first jobs are reliable.

            

              
Week 1: run the morning brief and two meeting briefs. Track what Cowork caught, missed, and over-prioritized.

              
Week 2: add follow-through automation for significant meetings and calibrate what counts as significant.

              
Week 3: add executive stakeholder and customer commitment tracking.

              
Week 4: add leadership update drafting and weekly risk review.
